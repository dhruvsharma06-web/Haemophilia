## Current Phase 1 baseline

The strongest observed model is the fresh-feature RBF SVM:

- Accuracy: 87.14%
- Balanced Accuracy: 83.87%
- Macro-F1: 85.18%
- Correct Recall: 94.54%
- Incorrect Recall: 73.20%

Confusion matrix:
`[[173, 10], [26, 71]]`

However, DO NOT treat this as a selected production model.

Major warning:

- 24/29 source videos contain only one quality class.
- Source-video/label Cramer's V = 0.9011.
- SVM source accuracy ranges from 30% to 100%.
- Source-level effects may still dominate generalization.
- Validation is source-grouped, not verified subject-held-out.

The purpose of Phase 2 is therefore NOT immediate optimization.

The purpose is to determine whether the current performance reflects genuine movement-quality information and to identify the main failure mechanisms.

# PHASE 2C — FEATURE ROBUSTNESS AUDIT

Audit every Phase 1 feature for susceptibility to source-specific artifacts.

Pay particular attention to:

- peak velocity
- mean velocity
- flare
- torso lean
- shoulder depth ratio
- phase duration
- start/end angle
- phase-derived speed
- bilateral asymmetry

For each feature determine:

1. physical interpretation;
2. units;
3. expected invariance to camera/view;
4. sensitivity to pose jitter;
5. sensitivity to missing landmarks;
6. sensitivity to repetition duration;
7. source-level distribution shift;
8. whether it could act as a recording-style proxy.

Do NOT remove features merely because they have outliers.

Recommend changes only where there is evidence of instability or source dependence.
